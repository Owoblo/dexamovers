Brand Identity / May 1, 2026

DEXA Movers

DEXA is positioned as a calm, capable, premium-feeling moving brand: practical enough for everyday moves, polished enough to feel trusted on uniforms, trucks, social pages, and printed materials.

The identity combines a deep navy base, a bright orange truck accent, and a bold sans-serif wordmark to communicate strength, care, and clear moving-day confidence.

Calm Capable Premium Local Modern

Brand Summary

Primary tagline: Heavy Lifting, Light Touch.

Campaign line: Move without the usual chaos.

Brand voice: direct, reassuring, organized, professional, never loud or gimmicky.

Visual signal: the oversized navy D carries a compact orange truck, making the moving category visible immediately.

Usage Direction

Use the full logo for the website, flyers, invoices, signage, and large-format print. Use the mark-only files for small placements like embroidery, favicons, social avatars, hat fronts, and icon badges.

Use reverse or one-color files when production limits the number of inks or when the background is dark, textured, or highly colored.

Logo System

Rule Guidance
Clear space Keep a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of one wheel.
Minimum size Full logo: 220 px digital or 1.75 in print. Mark only: 48 px digital or 0.5 in print.
Do not Stretch, recolor arbitrarily, add outlines, use drop shadows, or place on busy photography without a solid container.
Small production For embroidery or one-color printing under 3.25 in wide, use the mark-only file instead of the full lockup.
