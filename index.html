FAQ

Questions before
you book.

Straightforward answers to what people ask most before they commit to a move.

What types of moves do you handle? +

We handle local residential moves, long-distance moves, office relocations, packing help, loading and unloading, and labour-only jobs across Ottawa and surrounding areas.

Can I book packing help without a full move? +

Yes. Packing-only or partial packing support can be quoted separately from transport. Just select "Packing help" in the quote form.

Do you work with apartments, condos, and elevators? +

Yes. Use the notes field to flag stairs, elevator booking windows, parking issues, or loading dock rules so your crew arrives prepared.

How do I get a quote? +

Submit the quote form with your move type, date, and route. That gives enough context to start the conversation and confirm availability properly.

What areas of Ottawa do you serve? +

We serve Ottawa and the surrounding region, including Kanata, Nepean, Orleans, Barrhaven, Gloucester, Stittsville, Carleton Place, Kemptville, and Arnprior.
